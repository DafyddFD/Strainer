Four questions decide whether AI sends you the deal

A buyer's AI works through the same steps a good buyer always did. It just does them faster, before you're in the room.

  1. Can it find you?

    When a buyer describes their problem, are you in the answer at all?

  2. What does it say about you?

    Your strengths, your weaknesses, and whether any of it is still true.

  3. How do you stack up?

    Against your rivals, and against building it in-house or doing nothing.

  4. Do you hold up when they dig in?

    Pricing, integrations, security, and the fit questions buyers used to save for the demo.
