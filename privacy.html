Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Strainer LLC ("Strainer", "we") collects through getstrainer.com and what we do with it. Strainer LLC is registered in New Mexico, USA. Contact: dafydd@getstrainer.com.

What we collect

  • Benchmark requests: your website, email address, any competitors you name, and how you heard about us. Sent to us by email through FormSubmit.
  • Calls: if you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email and any details you add.
  • Site analytics: we use Plausible (cookie-free, aggregate statistics) and PostHog (product analytics, hosted in the EU) to understand how the site is used.
  • Company identification: we use RB2B to identify the companies, and in some cases the business contacts, that visit our site, so we can follow up with relevant businesses.

How we use it

  • To run and send the benchmark you asked for, and follow up about it.
  • To reply to your messages and run calls you book.
  • To understand and improve the site, and to contact businesses that may be interested in our services.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on your request (to provide the benchmark or a call) and on our legitimate interest in running and promoting our business. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers above, and email and hosting providers that help us run the business, each under their own terms. Some are based in the US, so your data may be processed outside the UK or EU.

How long we keep it

Benchmark requests and emails: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. Analytics: as set by each provider.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email dafydd@getstrainer.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority.
